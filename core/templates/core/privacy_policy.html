Privacy Policy

Pianivo - Mindful Scheduling
Last updated: August 23, 2026

Pianivo helps clients and businesses manage scheduling, bookings, support requests, and payments. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as name, email address, role, business code, and account creation date.
  • Booking information, such as service details, appointment date and time, customer name, and customer email.
  • Payment-related records, such as payment amount, currency, payment status, Stripe payment identifiers, and receipt email.
  • Support information you choose to send, including your message and optional app/device diagnostic details.

Payments

Card details and sensitive payment credentials are processed by Stripe. Pianivo does not store full card numbers, CVC codes, or raw payment credentials.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To schedule, confirm, reschedule, and manage appointments.
  • To process payment status updates and support refunds or payment questions.
  • To respond to support requests and troubleshoot app issues.
  • To protect the app, prevent abuse, and maintain reliable service.

Services We Use

Pianivo uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore for account/profile data, Stripe for payments, and Render to host backend services. These providers process information needed to operate the app.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers needed to run Pianivo, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. Some records may be retained when required for security, accounting, dispute handling, or legal obligations.
